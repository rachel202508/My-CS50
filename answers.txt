Q1 If songs.db contains the top 100 songs of one listener from 2018, how would you characterize their audio aura?
First, I would use the "SELECT" function to get the energy, valence, and danceability of the listener for all the songs.
Then, I would use the "AVG" function to calculate the average of the energy, valence, and danceability of the listener's top 100 songs from the past year.
After that, I can calculate the weight percentage to the two audio moods so that I can get the listener's "Audio Aura".

Q2 Hypothesize about why the way you’ve calculated this aura might not be very representative of the listener. What better ways of calculating this aura would you propose?
The audio aura may not be determined simply by energy, valence, and danceability.
We may calculate the average value of the tempo and key of the songs, as well.
